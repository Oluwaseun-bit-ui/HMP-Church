About HMP

A family of faith in Magodo, Lagos.

A family of faith in Magodo

Holiness Model Parish (HMP) is a parish of The Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG) in the Province of Ceaseless Praise, at Magodo Phase 1, Lagos.

We call ourselves The Holy Nation: a church where faith grows, love leads and impact lasts. Whether you are visiting for the first time or looking for a church home, you are welcome here.
